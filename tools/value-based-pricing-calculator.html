FAQ_04 // FREQUENTLY_ASKED_QUESTIONS
PRICING_QA
What is a standard Value Capture Percentage for agency proposals?

In professional services and technical consulting, agencies typically capture between 10% and 25% of the quantified first-year economic value created. 10% is standard for execution-heavy projects, 15%-20% for strategic transformations, and 25%+ for mission-critical risk mitigation.

Why do you still need to track time on value-based pricing contracts?

Even when billing a flat value fee, tracking internal delivery hours is mandatory. It is the only metric that reveals your Effective Hourly Rate (EHR = Value Fee / Total Hours) and true gross margin, ensuring project scope doesn't quietly erode profitability.

How do you present value-based pricing options to clients?

Always offer three tiered options (e.g., 10%, 18%, and 25% value capture). This shifts the client's decision from 'Should we hire this agency or not?' to 'Which tier of value and speed best fits our growth goals?'

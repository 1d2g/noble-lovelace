# VeloTime Product & Pricing Truth Invariants

Whenever generating, modifying, or auditing marketing copy, landing pages, blog posts, competitor comparisons, or SEO content for VeloTime, you MUST strictly adhere to the true pricing model and product offerings:

### 1. True Pricing Model
* **Flat Rate:** Exactly **$5 per user per month** (seat-based).
* **Zero Tiers:** Do NOT create artificial "Starter / Pro / Enterprise" pricing tiers.
* **Zero Add-On Taxes:** All platform features are included in every $5/user seat. No upcharges for reports, integrations, or admin seats.
* **Free Trial:** 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

### 2. True Product Offerings & Feature Set
* **10-Second Keyboard Matrix:** Spreadsheet-style bulk time entry with arrow keys.
* **Real-Time Project Profitability:** Gross margin tracking, loaded employee cost rates, and billing rates.
* **Billable Utilization Analytics:** Real-time percentage of billable vs non-billable hours.
* **Team Timesheet Review & Lock Approvals:** Manager approval workflows and period locks.
* **Invoicing & Stripe Integration:** Instant PDF generation and Stripe payment links for client billing.
* **Unbilled Expense Tracking:** Receipt attachment, expense classification, and automatic invoice linking.
* **Chrome Extension:** Lightweight popup widget with mini stopwatch and matrix sync.
* **100% Privacy-First:** Strict zero-surveillance policy (NO screenshots, NO keystroke logging, NO background window spying).
